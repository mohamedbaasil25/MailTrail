the gap this closes

Spam filters catch known patterns. They don't build a case.

A missed phishing email costs more than one inbox — it costs the hours a SOC analyst spends reconstructing what happened after the fact, and the window a victim has before money actually moves. MailTrail is built to shorten both: score the message the moment it lands, trace where it actually came from, and seal the finding into evidence before the trail goes cold.

One email, five checkpoints.

1

Ingest & parse

Headers and body are pulled apart — sender, links, attachments, and any SPF/DKIM/DMARC lines are isolated for scoring.

2

Multi-signal detection

Urgency language, credential lures, link and attachment risk, and authentication posture combine into one ensemble score.

Score 70+ continues to geo & evidence. Below that, the message is marked safe and the loop ends at the dashboard.

3

Geo attribution & impossible-travel check

The sender IP is traced and compared against that sender's last known location. A physically implausible jump feeds back into detection and raises the score again.

↪ feeds back into step 2

4

Evidence sealing

The message is hashed with SHA-256 and chained to the previous record, so an edit made after the fact would break the chain.

5

Forensic report & SOC alert

A CERT-In-formatted report compiles automatically and the dashboard updates in real time.

what's actually different

Four things this build gets right

01

Explainable by construction

Every point in the score traces to a named rule, not a hidden layer. An analyst sees exactly why an email was flagged, not just that it was.

02

Attribution, not just an IP lookup

Sender location is compared against where that sender was last seen, so an implausible jump between two messages gets caught — not just noted.

03

Evidence built for chain of custody

Every analysis is hashed and linked to the one before it, so the vault can prove nothing was altered after the fact — the blockchain half of the brief, done simply.

04

Tuned for the Indian threat surface

Bank and government brand look-alikes — .in domains, UIDAI-, and tax-department-style language — are checked specifically, not just generic kits built for other markets.

Running today, in this build

  • Heuristic ensemble engine — urgency, lures, link & attachment risk, brand look-alikes, auth-header parsing
  • Client-side SHA-256 evidence hashing, hash-chained into a custody log
  • Live GeoIP attribution, with a clearly labelled offline/reserved-range fallback
  • Impossible-travel detection across stored analyses
  • Printable, CERT-In-formatted forensic report
  • Node.js backend with file-based persistence — works fully offline too
